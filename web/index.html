Particle tracing

Blinded: images are shown in random order and orientation, without names.

  • Pen: trace the outer boundary of each particle, including its dark rim if it has one. Your line is used exactly as drawn and shows the diameter. Zoom in to trace precisely.
  • Tap a contour to mark it unsure or delete it.
  • Particles cut by the image edge: trace them anyway and carry the line past the edge where you think the outline goes. They are marked ~ (at edge).
  • Fingers: drag to move, pinch to zoom.
  • When everything in the image is traced, tap Approve. An image with no particles is approved empty.
  • Everything is saved automatically; you can stop and continue later.
